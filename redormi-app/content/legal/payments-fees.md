## 1. Redormi Rent

Guests pay the nightly rate, any applicable extra-guest and cleaning fees, a Redormi service fee (shown as a percentage of the subtotal before checkout), and applicable taxes. Hosts receive the nightly rate and cleaning fee, less Redormi's host service fee, paid out to their connected payout account after check-in.

## 2. Offers

An accepted offer or counter-offer locks in the discounted nightly rate shown at acceptance. Fees and taxes are recalculated on the discounted subtotal.

## 3. Redormi Switch

No rent is charged between Members. On mutual acceptance of a swap, each Member pays:

- A **processing fee**, calculated per stay length and shown in the swap proposal before either side pays.
- Any **add-ons** they opt into: liability coverage, damage protection, professional cleaning, verified key handoff, or cancellation protection.

Processing fees and add-ons are charged only once both Members have signed the Switch Agreement.

## 4. Extra services

Extra services (vehicles, recreation equipment, comfort items, and add-on services) are charged at the price and unit the Host or provider sets. Redormi takes a commission from the provider's payout, shown to Hosts when they create a service.

## 5. Payouts

Payouts to Hosts and service providers are issued through our payment partner to a connected bank account or card, typically within 1–3 business days after the payout is initiated.

## 6. Currency and taxes

Prices are shown in the currency selected in your account. Where required, Redormi collects and remits applicable taxes (such as occupancy or VAT) on behalf of Hosts; in other jurisdictions, Hosts are responsible for their own tax obligations.

## 7. Refund timing

Refunds are issued to the original payment method and can take 5–10 business days to appear, depending on your bank or card issuer.
