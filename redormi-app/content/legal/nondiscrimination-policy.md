## 1. Our commitment

Redormi welcomes Guests, Hosts, and Switch Members of every race, color, ethnicity, national origin, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, and age, in accordance with applicable law. Discrimination has no place in Rent bookings, Switch proposals, listing acceptance, offers, or reviews.

## 2. What this means in practice

- Hosts may not decline a booking or swap request, offer, or message based on a protected characteristic.
- Listings, house rules, and messages may not express a preference or requirement based on a protected characteristic, except where legally required (for example, gender-specific housing in narrowly defined circumstances).
- Automatic decision tools, such as offer auto-accept thresholds, must apply equally regardless of a guest's protected characteristics.

## 3. Accessibility

Hosts are encouraged to accurately describe accessibility features so Guests can make informed decisions, and to consider reasonable accommodations where feasible.

## 4. Reporting and enforcement

Report suspected discrimination through support with as much detail as possible. Confirmed violations can lead to a warning, listing removal, or account termination, and Redormi may require completion of a nondiscrimination review before reinstatement.
