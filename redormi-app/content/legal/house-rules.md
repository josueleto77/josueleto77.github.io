## 1. Purpose

These Guest Standards & House Rules ("House Rules") set the baseline of conduct expected on every Redormi Rent stay, in addition to any listing-specific rules a Host publishes on their listing page. They're modeled on the kind of standard terms used across the short-term rental industry (including Airbnb's own House Rules and Guest Standards policies), adapted for Redormi.

## 2. Booking accuracy

The name on a reservation must match the Guest who will actually stay. Guests may not book on behalf of someone else, resell a reservation, or exceed the maximum occupancy shown on the listing.

## 3. Check-in and check-out

Guests must respect the check-in and check-out windows shown on the listing and in their booking confirmation. Early check-in or late check-out is only permitted with the Host's advance approval, and may carry an additional fee set by the Host.

## 4. Respect the space and the neighbors

- No parties or events unless the listing explicitly allows them.
- Quiet hours (typically 10pm–8am local time, or as set by the Host) must be respected.
- Smoking is prohibited indoors unless the listing explicitly allows it.
- Pets are only allowed where the listing's amenities say "Pet friendly."
- Guests may not exceed the listed maximum occupancy.

## 5. Damage and reporting

Guests are responsible for damage they or their party cause beyond normal wear and tear, subject to the Host Guarantee & Damage Protection policy. Any damage, malfunction, or safety issue discovered during a stay should be reported to the Host through Redormi messaging as soon as possible so it can be addressed and so it isn't mistakenly attributed to the Guest at check-out.

## 6. Prohibited conduct

The following will result in removal from the platform and forfeiture of any refund protections: violence or threats toward a Host, Switch partner, or their property; unauthorized subletting; using a listing for illegal activity; hidden cameras or unauthorized surveillance of Guests by a Host (or vice versa); and any violation of the Nondiscrimination Policy.

## 7. Host obligations

Hosts must accurately represent their listing (photos, amenities, and location), maintain the property in a safe and habitable condition, and respond to Guest issues in good faith and in a timely manner, consistent with their stated response time.

## 8. Enforcement

Redormi may suspend or remove a Guest or Host account for a material violation of these House Rules, independent of any refund or damage process handled under the Cancellation Policies, Guest Refund Policy, or Host Guarantee.
