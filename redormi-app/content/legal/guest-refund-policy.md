## 1. Cancellation-based refunds

Refunds for a cancelled booking follow the cancellation policy shown on the listing at the time you booked — Flexible, Moderate, or Strict. See our Cancellation Policies document for what each tier guarantees.

## 2. Travel issues

You may be entitled to a refund or rebooking assistance, regardless of the listing's cancellation policy, if any of the following happen and you report them within 24 hours of check-in with supporting evidence:

- The Host cancels shortly before check-in.
- You're denied entry to the home and cannot reach the Host.
- The listing is materially different from its description (for example, a missing bedroom or advertised amenity that isn't present or working).
- The home is unsafe, unsanitary, or violates a material house rule you weren't told about.

## 3. How refunds are issued

Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method within 5–10 business days. Service fees are refunded in full when Redormi determines the Host is at fault, and are non-refundable for a standard Guest-initiated cancellation.

## 4. Extra services

Refunds for extra services (vehicles, recreation gear, comfort items, or add-on services) follow the cancellation policy set individually by the Host or provider for that service, shown at the time you add it.

## 5. Filing a claim

Report an issue through your trip page within 24 hours of discovering it. Photos, messages, and other documentation help us resolve claims quickly. Redormi's decision on a refund claim under this policy is final, without prejudice to any rights you have under local consumer law.
