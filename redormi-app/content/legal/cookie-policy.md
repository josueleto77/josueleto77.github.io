## 1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Redormi also uses similar technologies, like local storage, to remember information on your device between visits.

## 2. Why we use them

- **Essential** — keep you signed in, remember items in an in-progress booking or listing form, and protect against fraud. Redormi doesn't function without these.
- **Preferences** — remember settings like language, currency, and dismissed prompts, so you don't have to reset them every visit.
- **Analytics** — help us understand which pages and features are used, so we can fix what's broken and prioritize what matters.
- **Marketing** — measure the performance of Redormi's own promotions, such as Special Offers. Redormi does not sell cookie data to third-party advertisers.

## 3. First-party vs. third-party

Most cookies on Redormi are set directly by Redormi. Where we rely on a service provider — for example, a mapping or analytics provider — that provider may also set its own cookies, governed by its own policy, solely to deliver that feature to you.

## 4. How long cookies last

Session cookies are deleted when you close your browser. Persistent cookies remain for a set period (commonly 30 days to 12 months) or until you clear your browser storage, whichever comes first.

## 5. Managing your preferences

You can control non-essential cookies at any time from your browser settings, or from the cookie preferences link in Redormi's footer where available. Blocking essential cookies will prevent core features, like staying signed in or completing a booking, from working correctly.

## 6. Changes to this policy

We'll update this page if the cookies and technologies we use change materially, and will update the effective date above when we do.

## 7. Questions

Contact support if you have questions about how Redormi uses cookies or want help managing your preferences.
