## 1. Scope

This agreement applies whenever a Guest orders a vehicle-category Extra Service — including but not limited to cars, scooters, e-bikes, bicycles, kayaks, and boats — offered by a Host or third-party provider through Redormi.

## 2. Eligibility

A Guest renting a motorized vehicle (car, scooter, or similar) must hold a valid driver's license appropriate for that vehicle class, upload it for verification before pickup, and meet any minimum age set by the provider (25 years for cars unless the listing states otherwise). Non-motorized equipment (bicycles, kayaks, paddleboards) has no license requirement but may carry a minimum age or supervision requirement, shown on the listing.

## 3. Waiver of liability

Before pickup or use, the Guest must accept the provider's liability waiver for that specific item. The waiver acknowledges the inherent risks of operating a vehicle or using recreational equipment and releases the provider and Redormi from liability for injury or loss arising from ordinary use, to the extent permitted by law. Accepting the waiver does not waive rights that cannot be waived under applicable consumer-protection law.

## 4. Condition, fuel, and return

Guests must return a motorized vehicle with the same fuel level (or charge level, for electric vehicles) as at pickup, absent a different arrangement disclosed on the listing. Guests are responsible for traffic violations, tolls, and impound or towing fees incurred during the rental period. Equipment should be returned clean and in the condition it was received, ordinary wear excepted.

## 5. Damage and insurance

Damage beyond normal wear is billed to the Guest's payment method on file, up to the item's assessed repair or replacement cost, unless the Guest purchased optional damage protection at checkout. Guests are strongly encouraged to confirm whether their personal auto or travel insurance covers rental use before relying on it — Redormi does not guarantee any specific insurance coverage applies.

## 6. Prohibited use

Vehicles and equipment may not be used: outside the geographic area disclosed by the provider; to tow, race, or for any commercial purpose; while impaired by alcohol or drugs; or by anyone other than the verified renter, unless the listing explicitly allows additional drivers.

## 7. Cancellations

Vehicle and equipment Extra Services follow the cancellation window set by the individual provider on the service listing, shown at checkout before the Guest confirms the order.
