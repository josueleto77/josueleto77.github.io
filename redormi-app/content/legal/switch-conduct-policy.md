## 1. How this relates to the Home Exchange Agreement

The Home Exchange Agreement governs the terms of a specific swap once two members match — access, liability, insurance, and condition-on-return for that exchange. This policy sets the platform-wide safety and conduct standards every Switch Member agrees to simply by enabling Switch on their account, independent of any single swap.

## 2. Verification requirement

Redormi Switch requires a higher trust bar than a typical rental: every Switch Member must complete government-ID verification before they can propose or accept a swap. Members whose identity verification lapses or is revoked lose Switch access until it's restored.

## 3. Accurate listing of your home

A home offered for Switch must be accurately represented — the same standard as a Rent listing — including any shared spaces, house rules, neighborhood characteristics, and known maintenance issues that a reasonable swap partner would want to know before agreeing to stay there.

## 4. Conduct during a swap

Both parties in a swap are simultaneously a Host (of their own home) and a Guest (in their partner's home). The Guest Standards & House Rules apply to a Switch Member's conduct while staying in a partner's home. Additionally: Members must not invite guests beyond those disclosed when proposing the swap, must not use a swapped home as a base for commercial activity, and must respect the privacy of any personal belongings left in the home.

## 5. Safety reporting

A safety concern discovered before, during, or after a swap — including property misrepresentation, unsafe conditions, or a partner's conduct — should be reported to Redormi immediately through the swap's message thread and the in-app support option. Redormi may pause a swap, require additional verification, or remove a Member from Switch while a report is investigated.

## 6. Tier scoring is informational, not a guarantee

The Switch Tier shown on a listing reflects Redormi's assessment of a home's quality signals (photos, amenities, reviews, and verification status) to help members find comparably matched swaps. It is not an appraisal, an inspection, or a guarantee of condition — members are responsible for reviewing photos, listing details, and messaging their prospective partner before agreeing to a swap.

## 7. No-shows and disputes

If a Member fails to honor a confirmed swap without a covered exception, Redormi may suspend their Switch access and, where the affected party purchased the optional cancellation protection add-on, apply that protection per the Home Exchange Agreement. Disputes that can't be resolved between the two Members may be escalated to Redormi support for mediation.
