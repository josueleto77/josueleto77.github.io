## 1. Who this applies to

This Code of Conduct applies to every Redormi Member — Guests, Hosts, and Switch Members — in every interaction that happens through Redormi: messages, offers, reviews, support tickets, and any in-person contact arranged through a booking or swap.

## 2. Treat every Member with respect

- Communicate honestly and courteously, even when a booking, offer, or swap doesn't go the way you hoped.
- Show up for confirmed plans. If something changes, tell the other Member as early as possible.
- Respect a home, its neighbors, and its house rules as if they were your own.
- Disagree about a listing, price, or condition issue through Redormi's messaging and resolution tools rather than public harassment.

## 3. Zero tolerance for harassment and hate

Redormi has no tolerance for threats, hate speech, sexual harassment, or discrimination based on a protected characteristic, whether it happens in a message, a review, or in person. This includes conduct covered separately by our Nondiscrimination Policy, which this Code incorporates by reference.

## 4. Honesty and fair dealing

- Represent yourself, your home, and your intentions accurately — no fake listings, fake profiles, or misrepresenting who will be staying or exchanging.
- Don't attempt to move a confirmed booking or swap off-platform to avoid Redormi's fees or protections.
- Don't manipulate reviews, ratings, or the Switch Tier score through fake activity, coordinated voting, or pressure on the other party.

## 5. Safety first

Report anything that makes you feel unsafe — a threatening message, an unsafe property condition, or a Member who won't leave at checkout — through the in-app Report button or support, and contact local emergency services first if there's immediate danger.

## 6. Consequences

Violating this Code of Conduct can result in a warning, temporary suspension, removal of a listing or review, or permanent account termination, depending on severity and history, as described in our Terms of Service.

## 7. Reporting a concern

Use the Report option on the relevant message, listing, or profile, or contact support directly. We review conduct reports promptly and keep the reporter's identity confidential from the reported Member wherever possible.
