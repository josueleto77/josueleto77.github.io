## 1. Flexible

Guests receive a full refund if they cancel at least 24 hours before check-in. Cancelling later forfeits the first night, but the remaining nights are refunded.

## 2. Moderate

Guests receive a full refund if they cancel at least 5 days before check-in. Cancelling between 5 days and 24 hours before check-in refunds 50% of the total. No refund is guaranteed inside 24 hours.

## 3. Strict

Guests receive a 50% refund if they cancel at least 14 days before check-in. No refund is guaranteed inside 14 days, except where a covered Travel Issue applies under the Guest Refund Policy.

## 4. Choosing a policy

Hosts select one policy per listing from their listing settings. The policy shown to a Guest at the moment they book is the one that applies to that reservation, even if the Host later changes it for future bookings.

## 5. Extenuating circumstances

Regardless of the selected tier, Redormi may issue exceptions for documented emergencies — such as a serious illness, a declared natural disaster affecting the destination, or the death of the Guest or a close family member — at Redormi's discretion.

## 6. Redormi Switch

Because no rent is paid in a Switch exchange, these cancellation tiers don't apply to swaps. See the Home Exchange Agreement for how cancellations and no-shows are handled for Switch, including the optional cancellation protection add-on.
