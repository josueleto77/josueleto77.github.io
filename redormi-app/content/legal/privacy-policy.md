## 1. Information we collect

- **Account information**: name, email, phone number, date of birth, address, and profile photo.
- **Verification information**: government ID images, selfie verification, and the verification status they produce. We do not store raw ID images longer than needed to complete verification with our identity partner.
- **Payment information**: processed by our payment provider; Redormi stores only the last four digits and card type, or your linked payout account details for Hosts.
- **Usage information**: searches, saved homes, messages, offers, bookings, and swap proposals, used to operate the marketplace and to power features like last-minute deal alerts based on your recent searches.
- **Device information**: IP address, browser type, and general location, used for security and fraud prevention.

## 2. How we use information

We use your information to operate bookings and swaps, verify identity, process payments and payouts, personalize search and deal alerts, communicate with you about your account, and comply with legal obligations such as tax reporting.

## 3. How we share information

- With the other Member in a booking or swap, limited to what's needed to complete the stay (name, verification badges, and — once a booking or swap is confirmed — contact details).
- With service providers who process payments, verify identity, send email/SMS, or host our infrastructure, under contracts that limit their use of your data.
- With law enforcement or regulators when required by law.
- We do not sell your personal information.

## 4. Contact masking

Phone numbers and email addresses shared in messages are automatically masked until a booking or swap is confirmed, to protect both sides from being contacted or paid outside the platform.

## 5. Data retention

We retain booking, swap, and message records for as long as needed to resolve disputes, meet tax and accounting obligations, and improve safety — typically up to seven years after your last activity, unless a longer period is required by law.

## 6. Your choices

You can access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information from your account settings or by contacting support. Some information must be retained where required for legal, tax, or safety reasons, such as records of a completed swap or booking.

## 7. International transfers

Redormi may process data in countries other than where you live. Where required, we use standard contractual safeguards for cross-border transfers.

## 8. Children

Redormi is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We'll update the version and effective date above when this policy changes materially, and will notify you through the app or by email.
