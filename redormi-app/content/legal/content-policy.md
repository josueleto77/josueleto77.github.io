## 1. Accuracy

Listings, photos, and descriptions must accurately represent the home being offered — for Rent or Switch. Photos must be of the actual property, taken within the last 24 months, and not materially edited to hide defects.

## 2. Reviews

Reviews must reflect a genuine completed stay or exchange. Members may not offer or accept payment, discounts, or favors in exchange for a review, and may not threaten a bad review to extract concessions outside the offer or resolution systems. Redormi may remove reviews that violate this policy or contain content prohibited below.

## 3. Prohibited content

Content on Redormi — listings, photos, messages, profiles, and reviews — may not include: hate speech or content targeting a protected class; sexually explicit material; contact information shared to circumvent Redormi's fees before a booking or swap is confirmed; content that infringes someone else's intellectual property; or threats, harassment, or content promoting violence.

## 4. Enforcement

We may remove content that violates this policy, and repeated or severe violations can lead to listing suspension or account termination as described in these Terms.

## 5. Reporting

Report content that violates this policy from the listing, message thread, or review itself, or through support. We aim to review reports within 2 business days.
