Cancellation Policies

Version 1.0 · Effective Jan 15, 2026

[DRAFT — attorney review required]

1. Flexible

Guests receive a full refund if they cancel at least 24 hours before check-in. Cancelling later forfeits the first night, but the remaining nights are refunded.

2. Moderate

Guests receive a full refund if they cancel at least 5 days before check-in. Cancelling between 5 days and 24 hours before check-in refunds 50% of the total. No refund is guaranteed inside 24 hours.

3. Strict

Guests receive a 50% refund if they cancel at least 14 days before check-in. No refund is guaranteed inside 14 days, except where a covered Travel Issue applies under the Guest Refund Policy.

4. Choosing a policy

Hosts select one policy per listing from their listing settings. The policy shown to a Guest at the moment they book is the one that applies to that reservation, even if the Host later changes it for future bookings.

5. Extenuating circumstances

Regardless of the selected tier, Redormi may issue exceptions for documented emergencies — such as a serious illness, a declared natural disaster affecting the destination, or the death of the Guest or a close family member — at Redormi's discretion.

6. Redormi Switch

Because no rent is paid in a Switch exchange, these cancellation tiers don't apply to swaps. See the Home Exchange Agreement for how cancellations and no-shows are handled for Switch, including the optional cancellation protection add-on.

Listings, photos, and descriptions must accurately represent the home being offered — for Rent or Switch. Photos must be of the actual property, taken within the last 24 months, and not materially edited to hide defects.

We may remove content that violates this policy, and repeated or severe violations can lead to listing suspension or account termination as described in these Terms.

5. Reporting

Report content that violates this policy from the listing, message thread, or review itself, or through support. We aim to review reports within 2 business days.

Guest Refund Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective Jan 15, 2026

[DRAFT — attorney review required]

1. Cancellation-based refunds

Refunds for a cancelled booking follow the cancellation policy shown on the listing at the time you booked — Flexible, Moderate, or Strict. See our Cancellation Policies document for what each tier guarantees.

2. Travel issues

You may be entitled to a refund or rebooking assistance, regardless of the listing's cancellation policy, if any of the following happen and you report them within 24 hours of check-in with supporting evidence:

  • The Host cancels shortly before check-in.
  • You're denied entry to the home and cannot reach the Host.
  • The listing is materially different from its description (for example, a missing bedroom or advertised amenity that isn't present or working).
  • The home is unsafe, unsanitary, or violates a material house rule you weren't told about.

3. How refunds are issued

Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method within 5–10 business days. Service fees are refunded in full when Redormi determines the Host is at fault, and are non-refundable for a standard Guest-initiated cancellation.

4. Extra services

Refunds for extra services (vehicles, recreation gear, comfort items, or add-on services) follow the cancellation policy set individually by the Host or provider for that service, shown at the time you add it.

5. Filing a claim

Report an issue through your trip page within 24 hours of discovering it. Photos, messages, and other documentation help us resolve claims quickly. Redormi's decision on a refund claim under this policy is final, without prejudice to any rights you have under local consumer law.

Host Guarantee & Damage Protection

Version 1.0 · Effective Jan 15, 2026

[DRAFT — attorney review required]

1. What's covered

The Redormi Host Guarantee reimburses eligible Hosts for accidental damage a Guest or their invitees cause to a home or its contents during a confirmed Rent booking, up to the coverage limit shown in your Host dashboard at the time of the stay.

2. What's not covered

  • Normal wear and tear.
  • Damage caused by pets, unless Pet Friendly was enabled and pet damage coverage was active.
  • Cash, securities, rare art or collectibles, and similar high-value items unless separately declared.
  • Damage arising from a Host's own failure to disclose a known hazard.
  • Damage during a Redormi Switch exchange, which is instead covered by the Home Exchange Agreement's damage protection add-on, if purchased.

3. How to file a claim

Submit a claim through your Host dashboard within 14 days of checkout, with photos, an itemized cost estimate, and any supporting messages with the Guest. We may ask the Guest for their account of events before deciding a claim.

4. Income-loss protection

If a confirmed booking is cancelled by Redormi due to a policy violation by the Guest, or by the Guest within 48 hours of check-in without a covered Travel Issue, eligible Hosts may receive partial income-loss compensation for the affected nights, subject to review.

5. Security deposits

Hosts may also set a security deposit hold on a listing, charged to the Guest's payment method and released automatically after checkout unless a claim is filed under this Guarantee.

6. Limitations

The Host Guarantee is not an insurance policy. Total payouts per claim and per year are capped at the amounts shown in your Host dashboard, and claims are evaluated at Redormi's reasonable discretion.

Payments & Fees

Version 1.0 · Effective Jan 15, 2026

[DRAFT — attorney review required]

1. Redormi Rent

Guests pay the nightly rate, any applicable extra-guest and cleaning fees, a Redormi service fee (shown as a percentage of the subtotal before checkout), and applicable taxes. Hosts receive the nightly rate and cleaning fee, less Redormi's host service fee, paid out to their connected payout account after check-in.

2. Offers

An accepted offer or counter-offer locks in the discounted nightly rate shown at acceptance. Fees and taxes are recalculated on the discounted subtotal.

3. Redormi Switch

No rent is charged between Members. On mutual acceptance of a swap, each Member pays:

  • A processing fee, calculated per stay length and shown in the swap proposal before either side pays.
  • Any add-ons they opt into: liability coverage, damage protection, professional cleaning, verified key handoff, or cancellation protection.

Processing fees and add-ons are charged only once both Members have signed the Switch Agreement.

4. Extra services

Extra services (vehicles, recreation equipment, comfort items, and add-on services) are charged at the price and unit the Host or provider sets. Redormi takes a commission from the provider's payout, shown to Hosts when they create a service.

5. Payouts

Payouts to Hosts and service providers are issued through our payment partner to a connected bank account or card, typically within 1–3 business days after the payout is initiated.

6. Currency and taxes

Prices are shown in the currency selected in your account. Where required, Redormi collects and remits applicable taxes (such as occupancy or VAT) on behalf of Hosts; in other jurisdictions, Hosts are responsible for their own tax obligations.

7. Refund timing

Refunds are issued to the original payment method and can take 5–10 business days to appear, depending on your bank or card issuer.

Phone numbers and email addresses shared in messages are automatically masked until a booking or swap is confirmed, to protect both sides from being contacted or paid outside the platform.

6. Your choices

You can access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information from your account settings or by contacting support. Some information must be retained where required for legal, tax, or safety reasons, such as records of a completed swap or booking.

7. International transfers

Redormi may process data in countries other than where you live. Where required, we use standard contractual safeguards for cross-border transfers.

8. Children

Redormi is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We'll update the version and effective date above when this policy changes materially, and will notify you through the app or by email.
